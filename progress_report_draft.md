**Rationale & methods.** Stroke research usually treats anatomical (lesion), structural-disconnection (SDC), functional (fMRI) and physiological (EEG) signals as separate stories. This project (Corbetta Lab) tests instead whether they share a common latent structure: each modality is reduced to a low-dimensional embedding, clustered independently, and the resulting clusters cross-compared and related to behavioral outcomes (NIHSS, language, neglect, motor). Builds on evidence that lesion topography and disconnection are each intrinsically low-dimensional (Corbetta 2015; Thiebaut de Schotten et al. 2020; Talozzi et al. 2023), that disconnection explains cross-domain dysfunction better than raw lesion location (Griffis et al. 2019), and that lesion vs. FC predict different behavioral domains (Siegel et al. 2016). Five tasks: (1) lesion embedding/clustering (1150 validated, target ~5750); (2) SDC embedding (same cohort, in progress); (3) FC-based clustering (WashU) cross-referenced against the anatomical clusters; (4) EEG (~80, deferred to autumn 2026); (5) relating multimodal clusters to behavioral outcomes.

A layered pipeline harmonizes four cohorts into BIDS-Derivatives (WashU/PASPORT/PSP/UKLFR), builds a voxel-wise lesion matrix (1150 x 254,865), masks WashU's FC by lesion extent per Griffis et al. 2019 (50% node-coverage threshold) across 12 atlas combinations (Yan/Tian/Buckner, 100-400 cortical parcels), and orchestrates BCBToolKit for SDC.

Methodological choices so far: UMAP is the production reduction method (state of the art at this scale); t-SNE runs in parallel as a literature replication of Thiebaut de Schotten et al. 2020, sweeping perplexity as they do. On these binary masks, Jaccard/Dice distance outperforms Euclidean (trustworthiness ~0.70-0.74 to ~0.91-0.945) by normalizing for lesion size, but Euclidean is kept as a parallel analysis, since part of the Jaccard/Dice gain reflects a mechanical laterality split rather than new signal. Tuning sweeps the parameters expected to matter most (n_neighbors/min_dist for UMAP, perplexity for t-SNE); other methods are under literature review. PCA-varimax (Thiebaut de Schotten's exact methodology) is deliberately deferred until SDC is available, for direct comparison with Michele's own replication. Clustering (k-means, spectral, HDBSCAN, others under review) is fit on 2+ UMAP components; 2 components are used specifically for visualization.

**Progress & results.** Retrieval is stable/production. Embedding+clustering on lesions is done end-to-end with production visualizations (scatter plots colored by dataset/lesion side/volume, per-sample silhouette); UMAP identifies a robust k~4 (KMeans/GMM-confirmed), while plain PCA is volume-confounded (r=0.92 with PC2) and excluded. FC masking is complete for the full WashU cohort (169/202 subjects with both lesion and FC, all 12 atlas combinations); FC is now ready for its own embedding+clustering. Cross-modal comparison metrics (ARI/NMI, contingency tables) are implemented but not yet run, pending SDC. BCBToolKit is validated end-to-end on a real subject; full-cohort SDC is blocked on a complete tractography reference atlas.

**Difficulties.** Caught before/after production: a scoring function silently using the wrong distance metric; a plotting bug slicing multi-dimensional embeddings as if PCA-ordered; silent third-party bugs (integer overflow, dropped regions). BCBToolKit's cluster-only dependency needed a non-standard local install (server permission request pending).

**Future steps.** Launch full-scale SDC; compute ARI/NMI between lesion, SDC and FC clusters; extend interpretive visualizations (lesion probability maps, atlas overlap, centroid radar plots); characterize clusters against NIHSS/behavioral domains; run consensus/stability clustering to validate cluster counts.
